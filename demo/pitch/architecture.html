Architecture

Webcam

Laptop camera or an uploaded video

MediaPipe

Holistic: 543 landmarks per frame

Segmenter

Hands up to hands down 0.7 s; resample to 30 fps

Features

Hands + 7 body points, normalised: 198 per frame

BiGRU + CTC

Conv1D, 2 BiGRU layers, 2.38M parameters

Text

Greedy decoding; words under 0.6 confidence flagged

CTC loss

Learns where each sign starts and ends from sentence labels alone; no frame-level annotation needed.

8 ms per second of signing

Recognition latency on a laptop CPU, one thread; 77 ms for a 10-second sentence. MediaPipe at about 11 fps is the slower stage.

Confidence per word

Mean probability over the frames that produced the word, with the top-3 alternatives for correction.

Training: AdamW, one-cycle schedule, mixed precision, 25 epochs of about 42 s on a Kaggle T4 · Augmentation: rotation, scale, shift, noise, brief hand dropouts

Every frame becomes 543 landmarks; we keep both hands and seven upper-body points. The segmenter waits for the hands to drop for 0.7 seconds, then the whole sentence goes through the model at once. CTC is the key choice: it aligns frames to words on its own. The model is small enough that recognition takes single-digit milliseconds; the landmark extraction is what limits the frame rate.
