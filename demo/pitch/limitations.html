What it cannot do yet

52 ASL signs, nothing else

There is no "not a sign" option. An unknown gesture is mapped to the closest known sign, usually with low confidence.

Synthetic sentences

Test sentences are joined isolated clips with interpolated transitions. Real co-articulation is only approximated.

Live accuracy unmeasured

Trained on phone videos with the older MediaPipe; the app uses a laptop webcam and MediaPipe Tasks. Live WER: [not yet measured].

Hands and upper body only

Facial expressions carry grammar in sign languages. The model never sees the face.

One seed, three test signers

The normalisation ablation (21.0% against 24.6%) is consistent but has not been repeated across random seeds.

Glosses, not English

Output follows sign order, such as "mom drink water now". Translation into Tamil, Hindi and Telugu is future work.

Processing stays on the laptop and no video is stored · This prototype must not replace a qualified interpreter

Be direct here: judges reward honest negatives. The biggest gap is out-of-vocabulary gestures: in testing, a raised middle finger came out as "see", because the model has to pick from 52 signs. Live accuracy is the number we most want and do not yet have; give the demo's live WER as it is.
